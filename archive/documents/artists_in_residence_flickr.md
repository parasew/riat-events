# Artists-in-Residence Documentation - Flickr Archive

## Overview
The artisticbokeh Flickr account contains extensive documentation of artists-in-residence and related events from the RIAT/Artistic Bokeh/5uper.net programs.

## Flickr Account Details
- **Account**: [artistic bokeh](https://www.flickr.com/photos/artisticbokeh/)
- **Photos**: 2,063 images
- **Joined**: 2012
- **Primary Content**: Event documentation, exhibition photos, artist portraits, installation shots

## Artists-in-Residence Timeline from Flickr Evidence

- **Context**: Continued artist collaborations and exhibitions
