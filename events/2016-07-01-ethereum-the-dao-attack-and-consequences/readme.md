## Historical Context: The DAO Attack

### The DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)
- **Launch**: April 30, 2016
- **Purpose**: Venture capital fund governed by token holders
- **Funding**: Raised approximately $150 million in ETH (largest crowdfunding at the time)
- **Significance**: Flagship application demonstrating Ethereum's smart contract capabilities

### The Attack
- **Date**: Attack began June 17, 2016
- **Vulnerability**: Recursive call bug in DAO smart contract
- **Mechanism**: Attacker exploited "splitDAO" function to drain funds repeatedly
- **Impact**: Approximately 3.6 million ETH (worth ~$50 million at the time) siphoned

### Immediate Aftermath
- **Community crisis**: Major test for Ethereum's governance and security
- **Technical response**: Proposal for hard fork to recover funds
- **Philosophical debate**: "Code is law" vs. community intervention
- **Market impact**: Significant ETH price drop and uncertainty
