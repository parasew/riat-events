# Urban Knights Vienna

## Event Details

- **Date**: July 3, 2014
- **Location**: paradocks, Marxergasse 24, 1030 Vienna, Austria
- **Organizers**: Artistic Bokeh in partnership with Urban Knights, quartier21 artist-in-residency programme, and paradocks
- **Type**: Event / Discussion
- **Curated by**: Teresa Dillon
- **Facebook Event**: [1435618953366931](https://www.facebook.com/events/1435618953366931/)

## About Urban Knights

**Urban Knights** is a programme of events that provokes and promotes practical approaches to urban change. Established in Dublin, the programme brings together people who are actively creating alternatives that reflect or produce disruptions to our given city infrastructures, norms, and perceptions.

### Vienna Edition Focus
This first edition in Vienna focused on:
- **New urban governance**
- **Commons and collective well-being**
- **Shared civic resources**
- **Alternative urban infrastructures**

## Speakers

### 1. Julia Wieger [AT]
- **Architect**
- **Co-organiser** of Vienna's Summer School for 'Spaces of Commoning'
- **Founder** of the 'Radical Practices of Collective Care' project
- **Presentation**: Developing cooperative care structures for citizens

### 2. Hans Bernhard [AT]
- **Co-founder** of the internationally renowned artist group [UBERMORGEN](https://ubermorgen.com/)
- **Focus**: Rethinking approaches to governance
- **Presentation**: Radical self-experiments, including media and legal hacks as strategies for rethinking how we organize our techno-civic lives

### 3. Theresa Schütz [AT]
- **Architect**
- **Co-founder** of the urban arts collective 'Gehsteig-Guerrilleros'
- **Member** of the CIT Collective
- **Presentation**: Lobbying to turn Vienna's 'Gaswork Leopoldau' into an open and democratically run area for cultural development and production
