# [Coded Cultures Openism] Edit-a-thon

## Event Details

- **Date**: May 22, 2016
- **Time**: 13:00 (1:00 PM)
- **Location**: Coded Cultures Central, Vienna
- **Host**: Mz* Baltazar's Laboratory (feminist hackerspace collective)
- **Event**: Edit-a-thon (feminist Wikipedia editing workshop)
- **Series**: Coded Cultures Openism festival
- **Audience**: Women*, Inter*, Trans* (FLIT) folk only (safer space)
- **Format**: Drop-in workshop with food and drinks provided

## Event Description

Wikipedia seems like an open, participatory platform, but in fact 90 percent of its editors are male.

At the EDIT-A-THON hosted by Mz* Baltazar's Laboratory you can drop in and find out how to publish and how to edit an article, add information about women in technology/art/activism or even develop your own Wikipedia article!

## About Edit-a-thons

### Origins and Format
To encourage a representative number of articles from a female perspective, the US-based feminist organization 'art+feminism' created a format called 'Edit-a-thon'. It has its roots in the term 'hacker-thon', a night and day-long working session among hackers who try to collaboratively get Open Source projects done.

### Global Feminist Wikistorming
The 'Edit-a-thon' in Vienna was part of a networked wikistorming during which feminists on different locations in the world reclaimed Wikipedia! This event aimed to become the most intense working session of feminist article writing ever in Vienna.

## Workshop Content

### Learning Wikipedia Editing
Participants learned how to:
- **Publish and edit articles** on Wikipedia
- **Add information** about women in technology, art, and activism
- **Develop new articles** from scratch
- **Navigate Wikipedia's structure** and editorial policies

### Discussion Topics
The workshop included discussions about:
- **Wikipedia structure**: How the platform is organized
- **Controversial issues**: How they are handled on Wikipedia
- **Global perspectives**: Is it possible to give a global perspective on Wikipedia?
- **Editorial processes**: How many changes are done each minute? Who checks information?
- **Editorial rules**: What rules do Wikipedia editors follow?

## About Mz* Baltazar's Laboratory

### Feminist Hackerspace Collective
Mz* Baltazar's Laboratory is a collective and feminist hackerspace that aims at:
- **Generating a culture of fearless making**
- **Fostering creativity, activism and provocative thinking**
- **Building an accessible, inclusive, open, safer and radical space**
- **Enabling personal and community evolution**

### Philosophy
Open Source Technology is at the root of their philosophy, enabling sharing and collaboration without restrictions. The space allows experimentation with gender, hardware, and personal identity.
