# Public Lecture – Melentie Pandilovski "New Media Art in Australia"

## Details

- **Date:** November 10, 2005
- **Time:** 18:00-20:00
- **Location:** 5uper.net Cuisine Digitale, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net
- **Type:** Public Lecture
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/129.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20150209174810/https://5uper.net/post/129.html)

## Description

Melentie Pandilovski, Director of the Experimental Art Foundation in Adelaide, Australia, presents a lecture on "New Media Art in Australia" at the 5uper.net Cuisine Digitale in MuseumsQuartier Vienna.

The lecture provides an overview of Australian and international artists and their works related to "Art of the Biotech Era," a 2004 art project that critically examines the impacts and changes brought by biotechnology science.

## Speaker

**Melentie Pandilovski** - Curator and Director of the [Experimental Art Foundation](http://www.eaf.asn.au/) in Adelaide, Australia.

## Content

The presentation examines artists' engagement with biotechnology:
- Critical examination of gene splicing and chromosomal experiments
- Ethical judgments and imaginative engagement with biotechnological changes
- Artists' responses to industrial farming, genetic modification, and biological interventions

## Quotes

"Artists from around Australia & the world showed work examining, critiquing, speculating within, the sphere of the Biotechnological. This nascent science—in its more interventionist forms of gene splicing and chromosomal experiment—might alter the face of the world as we know it & will require ethical judgements and imaginative engagement if these changes are to be safely guided and met with."
