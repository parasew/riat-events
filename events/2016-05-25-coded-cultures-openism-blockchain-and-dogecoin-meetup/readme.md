## Workshop Content

### Dogecoin as Gateway
- **Dogecoin background**: Created in 2013 as joke cryptocurrency based on Doge meme
- **Community culture**: Emphasis on fun, generosity, and internet culture
- **Technical foundation**: Based on Litecoin codebase with faster block times
- **Educational value**: Lower stakes entry point for learning about cryptocurrency

### Blockchain Fundamentals
Workshop likely covered:
- **How blockchain works**: Distributed ledger technology basics
- **Cryptocurrency mechanics**: Transactions, mining, wallets
- **Dogecoin specifics**: Technical characteristics and community aspects
- **Practical experience**: Setting up wallets, making transactions

### Cultural Dimensions
Discussion probably included:
- **Internet culture and finance**: Memes, communities, and value creation
- **Alternative economics**: Dogecoin's philanthropic and community-focused use
- **Serious play**: Using humor and play to explore serious technology
- **Accessibility**: Making complex technology approachable through cultural framing
