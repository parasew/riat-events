### Description
In the Artist-In-Residence studios at MQ Vienna, Artistic Technology Research presented the results of the cooperation with the Utrecht Media Lab SETUP. Both groups were involved in mapping collaboration and communication within networks of creative producers. Mailing lists, Twitter interactions, search queries and online repositories represent unstable and often neglected repositories of social online interaction.

By using this data for visualizations and for a representation of the development of communication and interaction, it became clear how online discourses develop, which actors are important and what stories the traces of online life can tell about contributors and social formations. The results are data analyses, network visualizations and infographics that make explicit the implicit interactions as well as the often neglected “digital footprints” of internet use. Exhibited were data portraits by Frank-Jan van Lunteren, network visualizations by Ryanne Turenhout and data analyses by Matthias Tarasiewicz.

The event also featured the **2012 Artistic Technology Colloquium #1** with Bernd Kräftner, Matthias Tarasiewicz, Lorenz Seidler, Roman Kirschner, and Mirko T. Schäfer.
