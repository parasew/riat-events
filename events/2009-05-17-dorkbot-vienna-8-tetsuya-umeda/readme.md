## Description

The 5uper.net artist-in-residence Tetsuya Umeda presents his work as part of Dorkbot Vienna #8 at Metalab Vienna.

## About Tetsuya Umeda

"Altering fans, televisions, and other everyday appliances, converting their functions into something utterly different, UMEDA creates objets freed from their original meanings. His works, created to suit the spaces in which they will be installed, use utterly familiar, commonplace phenomena, channeled through his clever handiness and unique spatial sensibility, to create mysterious, seemingly impossible sensations. They thereby free one from rigid perceptions constrained by preconceived ideas."

Tetsuya Umeda is an artist currently working in Osaka, Japan. He has done several sound installation projects and performances that generate site-specific soundscapes using minimal function of electric circuits/systems and simple physical phenomena.

## Background

Umeda has performed at Festival Beyond Innocence, an experimental music festival in Japan, since 2002. In 2006, he performed at the internationally acclaimed underground music event INSTAL in Scotland, UK. He has also participated in installation/exhibition projects including The Listening Project (London-based residency program, 2006), Sound Effect Seoul (2007), and Waitool Sounds project in San Francisco (2007).
