# [Research Cultures] dérive: Radical Utopia? La Revolution urbaine

- **Opening Date**: August 20, 2014, 19:00
- **Exhibition Period**: August 20 - September 22, 2014
- **Location**: Artistic Bokeh Showroom, Electric Avenue, quartier21, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Address**: Museumsplatz 1, 1070 Vienna, Austria
- **Organizers**: Artistic Bokeh in cooperation with dérive - Association for Urban Research
- **Type**: Exhibition
- **Website**: [quartier21 Event Page](https://www.quartier21.at/programm/programmdetail/derive-radical-utopia-la-revolution-urbaine/)

## About the Exhibition

**"Radical Utopia? La Revolution urbaine"** was an exhibition showcasing the multidimensional, socio-political dialogue with urban space developed by dérive - Association for Urban Research over 14 years.

### Exhibition Concept
The exhibition presented a selection of dérive's work exploring urban society, where the inhabitants of the city, their needs, desires, and actions are at the center of focus. The title references Henri Lefebvre's 1967 statement: **"Urban life has not even begun."**

### Content Highlights
The exhibition featured:
- **dérive magazine**: Publications on urban research and theory
- **urbanize festival**: Documentation of the international festival for urban exploration
- **urbanize radio**: Audio content and interviews
- **Video lectures**: Educational and discussion content
- **"laboratoire dérive" urban game**: Interactive exploration of urban spaces

### Philosophical Context
The "City" as social space is created daily through constant negotiation in political debates and urban action. dérive serves as a platform intersecting science, art, and activism while working toward realizing an urban society centered on inhabitants' needs and desires.

## About dérive - Association for Urban Research

**dérive** is a Vienna-based association dedicated to urban research that:
- Intersects science, art, and activism
- Publishes the dérive magazine on urban topics
- Organizes the urbanize International Festival for Urban Exploration
- Produces urbanize radio content
- Develops educational tools like the "laboratoire dérive" urban game

## Connection to Artistic Bokeh

Artistic Bokeh invited dérive to present their work as part of the Research Cultures series, exploring how artistic and research practices can engage with urban spaces and social structures.
