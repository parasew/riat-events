# "INSERT COIN"

**Kompexitätsbewährung im neoliberalen Kontext - 9 Arkade-Spielautomaten im Freiraum/ MQ Wien**

## Details

- **Date:** May 28, 2005 (Opening)
- **Time:** 19:00
- **Location:** Freiraum, MuseumsQuartier Wien
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net
- **Type:** Exhibition Opening
- **Website:** [insertcoin.biz](http://insertcoin.biz)

## Description

An exhibition featuring 9 arcade game machines exploring complexity within neoliberal contexts. Presented by 5uper.net in the Freiraum space of Vienna's MuseumsQuartier.

## Context

Part of 5uper.net's early activities in the MuseumsQuartier, exploring the intersection of gaming culture, technology, and socio-economic critique through interactive installations.

## Notes

- The exhibition opening was on May 28, 2005 at 19:00
- Featured 9 custom arcade game machines
- Explored themes of complexity and neoliberal contexts
