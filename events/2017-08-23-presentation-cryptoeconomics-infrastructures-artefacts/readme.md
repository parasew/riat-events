# [Presentation] Cryptoeconomics: Infrastructures & Artefacts

## Event Details

- **Date**: August 21, 2017
- **Time**: 21:00 (9:00 PM)
- **Location**: Hallenbad Alpbach, Alpbach, Austria
- **Event**: BLOCKCHAIN evening hosted by Club Alpbach Oberösterreich
- **Organizer**: RIAT Research Institute for Arts & Technology
- **Host**: Club Alpbach Oberösterreich in cooperation with Federal Ministry of Science, Research and Economy
- **Program**: Cryptoeconomics: Infrastructures & Artefacts
- **Type**: Presentation and exhibition of blockchain art and research

## Event Description

RIAT Research Institute for Arts & Technology presents its **Cryptoeconomics: Infrastructures & Artefacts** program at the BLOCKCHAIN evening hosted by Club Alpbach Oberösterreich in cooperation with the Federal Ministry of Science, Research and Economy.

## Program Overview

### Insights into Crypto-Economic Condition
Cryptoeconomics: Infrastructures & Artefacts offers insights into the crypto-economic condition and presents an overview of blockchain-related activities within Austria from 2011-2017.

### Featured Projects

#### 1. PROOF-OF-PRESENCE
- **Developed by**: DARC and the NEM Foundation
- **Concept**: Critically questions utopian/dystopian narratives of machine labour with a performative blockchain
- **Approach**: Artistic exploration of blockchain's implications for labor and presence

#### 2. Bitcoincloud
- **Creator**: Artistic Bokeh
- **Significance**: One of the earliest blockchain-based artworks
- **Context**: Exhibited as historical example of blockchain art in Austria
- **Timeline**: Originally created around 2011-2012

#### 3. Terra0 Project
- **Concept**: A self-owned forest that creates a scenario whereby a forest is able to sell licences to log trees through automated processes, smart contracts and blockchain technology
- **Presenter**: Hmpshr Mx, RIAT's current crypto-economic artist and researcher-in-residence with Q21 at MQ – MuseumsQuartier Wien
- **Innovation**: Exploration of autonomous economic entities and decentralized resource management

## RIAT's Cryptoeconomics Program

Cryptoeconomics: Infrastructures & Artefacts is an ongoing program by RIAT that was featured in various iterations over the following year, including:

- **The Future of Demonstration** exhibition/event series

### Program Philosophy
The program represents RIAT's approach to cryptoeconomics as a field that intersects:
- **Artistic practice** and technological innovation
- **Economic systems** and cultural critique
- **Research development** and public engagement
- **Historical context** and future speculation

## About RIAT (2017)

### Institutional Focus
In 2017, RIAT defined itself as:
"An institute for research, development, communication and education in the fields of cryptoeconomics, the blockchain, experimental artistic technology and open hardware."

### Mission and Approach
RIAT's mission included:
- **Exploring and stress-testing** the role of research and development in the age of zero-trust
- **Developing novel forms** of presentation, discussion and publication
- **Examining the global cryptoeconomic condition** and its effects on culture and society
- **Fostering open and interdisciplinary discourse** to improve crypto-literacy for the society of tomorrow
