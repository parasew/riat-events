## About the Event

This **Design Culture Salon** discussion explored how design practices and methodologies contribute to the creation and development of alternative economies in contemporary culture.

### Discussion Themes
- **Design as economic intervention**: How design thinking and practice can create alternative economic models
- **Alternative economies**: Exploration of non-traditional economic systems (sharing economies, gift economies, crypto economies)
- **Cultural production**: The role of design in shaping cultural and economic value
- **Contemporary challenges**: Addressing economic inequalities and system failures through design
