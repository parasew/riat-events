## Exhibition Details

- **Exhibition Period**: November 29, 2013 - January 5, 2014
- **Location**: MAK – Museum of Applied Arts, Stubenring 5, 1010 Vienna
- **Organizer**: University of Applied Arts Vienna
- **Type**: Exhibition
- **Website**: [MAK Exhibition Page](https://www.mak.at/en/program/exhibitions/out_of_the_box)

## About the Exhibition

**"Out of the Box"** presented a broad spectrum of recent research practices in which knowledge is produced through artistic approaches. The exhibition featured snapshots from ten international research projects of the University of Applied Arts Vienna that break open the borders between art and science.

### Exhibition Concept
The exhibition explored artistic research as a methodology that produces knowledge through artistic practice, challenging traditional boundaries between art and science.

### Featured Projects
The exhibition included ten research projects from the University of Applied Arts Vienna, representing diverse approaches to artistic research across various disciplines.

### Artistic Bokeh Involvement
Artistic Bokeh was involved in documenting and presenting aspects of the exhibition, with photographic documentation available in their Flickr archive.

## Context

### Artistic Research Movement
The exhibition was part of a growing international movement recognizing artistic research as a valid form of knowledge production, with institutions like the University of Applied Arts Vienna at the forefront of this development.

### University of Applied Arts Vienna Research
The exhibition showcased the university's commitment to research that integrates artistic practice with academic inquiry, highlighting innovative approaches to knowledge production.

"Out of the Box" represented an important exhibition in the development of artistic research as a recognized field, showcasing how artistic practice can contribute to knowledge production and challenging traditional divisions between art and science. The exhibition highlighted the University of Applied Arts Vienna's leadership in this emerging field.
