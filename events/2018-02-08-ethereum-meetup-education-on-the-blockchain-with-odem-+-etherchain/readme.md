# Ethereum Meetup - Education on the blockchain with ODEM + Etherchain

## Event Details

- **Date**: February 8, 2018
- **Time**: 18:00 (6:00 PM)
- **Location**: RIAT Vienna (likely Wilhelminenstrasse 121 or related space)
- **Organizer**: Ethereum Vienna meetup group, hosted at RIAT
- **Type**: Technical meetup with presentations and discussion
- **Speakers**: 
  - Emilie Raffo (ODEM / Blockchain 4 Good, Switzerland)
  - Peter from Bitfly (Etherchain.org)
- **Series**: First Ethereum Vienna meetup of 2018

## Event Description

It's the first meetup of the year. This time we are joined by Emilie from ODEM and Peter from Bitfly. First we will have a quick update about things related to Ethereum. Then Emilie will talk about ODEM. Finally Peter will talk about etherchain and some metrics of the blockchain.

## Agenda

### 1) Ethereum Updates
A quick update about recent happenings in the Ethereum world.

### 2) ODEM - On-Demand Education Marketplace
**Speaker**: Emilie Raffo, organizer of the Swiss "Blockchain 4 Good" meetup

**Topic**: The use of blockchain technology to make education accessible to all. She will present the use case of ODEM: the "On-Demand Education Marketplace" that leverages Blockchain technology and AI to offer the best contents and certifications to ALL students, whatever their net-worth, and whatever their origin.

### 3) Exploring the Ethereum Blockchain
**Speaker**: Peter from Bitfly

**Topic**: This talk gives a look behind the scenes of developing and operating etherchain.org, the first Ethereum Blockchain Explorer. Furthermore we discuss and analyze key metrics of the Ethereum network collected by the Blockchain explorer and give an in depth view on the challenges faced in scaling Ethereum from the point of view of a mining pool.
