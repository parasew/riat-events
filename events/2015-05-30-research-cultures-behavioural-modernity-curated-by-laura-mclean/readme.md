## Theme: Behavioural Modernity

### Conceptual Framework
**Behavioural modernity** refers to the set of traits that distinguish present-day humans from earlier hominids and other primates. In contemporary discourse, it often explores:

- **Cognitive evolution**: Development of modern human behavior
- **Cultural transmission**: How behaviors and knowledge are passed through generations
- **Symbolic thought**: Development of language, art, and symbolic systems
- **Technological innovation**: Relationship between behavior and tool use

### Artistic and Research Perspectives
The event likely explored how artistic practice and research can engage with and reinterpret concepts of behavioral modernity, potentially including:

- **Contemporary behaviors**: Modern human behaviors in digital age
- **Cultural evolution**: How behaviors evolve in contemporary societies
- **Art as behavioral research**: Artistic practice as investigation of human behavior
- **Interdisciplinary approaches**: Combining anthropology, art, and cognitive science
