# Launch: Runway Experimental Australian Arts Issue 26: Knowledge (Edited by Andrew Newman)

## Event Details

- **Date**: November 20, 2014
- **Time**: 19:00
- **Location**: MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Coordinates**: 48°12'15.4"N 16°21'29.2"E
- **Organizers**: Artistic Bokeh and Austria Australia Arts Alliance
- **Type**: Launch Event
- **Context**: Part of Vienna Art Week 2014
- **Facebook Event**: [1006015856079121](https://www.facebook.com/events/1006015856079121/)

## About the Event

Artistic Bokeh and the Austria Australia Arts Alliance presented the launch of **Runway Australian Experimental Art Issue #26: Knowledge** alongside the opening of the Austria Australia Arts Alliance exhibition program.

### Event Components
1. **Launch of Runway Issue #26: Knowledge** edited by Andrew Newman
2. **Presentation by Dr. Joshua Harle** (Australian artist and researcher)
3. **Music by Soulpeeling**
4. **Drinks and networking**

## Runway Journal Issue #26: Knowledge

**Runway** is an independent Australian experimental art journal run by a collective of Sydney-based and internationally-based artists, writers, and curators.

### Issue Theme: Knowledge
The issue reflects on how knowledge is generated within artistic practice, exploring the intersection of art, research, and knowledge production.

## Austria Australia Arts Alliance (AAAA)

The **Austria Australia Arts Alliance** is an organization established to present and promote emerging experimental Australian artists in Austria. This event launched its one-year exhibition program at Artistic Bokeh in partnership with Runway Australian Experimental Art.

## Context: Vienna Art Week 2014

The event was part of **Vienna Art Week 2014**, positioning Australian experimental art within the Vienna art scene and fostering international artistic exchange.
