# Deutz Air Special (Köln) Party!

## Details

- **Date:** June 2-6, 2006
- **Location:** 5uper.net Cuisine Digitale, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net
- **Type:** Exhibition & Party
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/149.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20150209174810/https://5uper.net/post/149.html)

## Description

Three Cologne projects from different areas present themselves in Vienna under the name of the venue Deutz Air.

## Participating Projects

### 1. John Harten with PUBLIC FOLDER
A serial book project that collects contributions from known and unknown international artists on specific topics. The PUBLIC FOLDER group exhibition on the theme of football (pictures on the wall and video) is shown and the book is presented.

"In their captivating new PUBLIC FOLDER, John Harten, Bernd Kleinheisterkamp, Sonia Alvarez and Sven Lison manage to further intensify the tension of the quietly smoldering disaster and at the same time, almost incidentally, tell the story of a great love - football."

- **Website:** [public-folder.com](http://www.public-folder.com/)

### 2. RAUM FÜR PROJEKTION
"Raum für Projektion" is a DVD label and temporary playback location for video projects. A selection from the DVD project "loop pool 05" is shown, for which international artists, filmmakers and VJs each produced a video loop. "loop pool" is a project by Graw Böckler Heydebreck in cooperation with the International Short Film Festival Oberhausen.

- **Website:** [raumfuerprojektion.de](http://www.raumfuerprojektion.de/)

### 3. POPNONAME
Releases on the labels FIRM, ITALIC and KOMPAKT, writes theater music for various German stages, film music, pop music and techno - currently the remix for Alessandro Crimi (italic 58) and a remix (with John Harten) for Modernist on the SPEX 72 EP. Currently working on a new mini-LP for Firm.

POPNONAME prepares an ambient set for the exhibition opening, as well as a later techno live act for the "icke micke" club.

- **Website:** [popnoname.de](http://www.popnoname.de/)
- **Club:** [ickemicke.at](http://www.ickemicke.at/)

## Context

Part of 5uper.net's program presenting international collaborations and cross-disciplinary projects in the Cuisine Digitale space.
