## About the Artist

**Georgios Papadopoulos** is a Greek artist and researcher based in Berlin who combines economics and philosophical analysis with exploratory artistic practice. His research focuses on money and its socioeconomic functions.

### Education & Background
- **PhD**: Erasmus University, Rotterdam
- **Master**: Philosophy of the Social Sciences, London School of Economics
- **Research Fellow**: Theory department, Jan Van Eyck Academy, Maastricht (2008-2009)
- **Award**: Vilém Flusser Residency for Artistic Research (2012)

### Publications
- **Book**: *Notes towards a Critique of Money* (2011)
- **Edited Volume**: *Grexit* (2013)
- **Articles**: Published in scientific journals, art reviews, and edited volumes in English, Greek, and Spanish

### Artistic Practice
Papadopoulos' artistic practice explores the intersection of economics, philosophy, and visual culture. He has presented work internationally at venues including:
- Kunsthalle Athena, Athens
- Transmediale, Berlin
- quartier21, Vienna
- Aarhus Kunsthal, Aarhus
- Le Plateau, Paris
- Athens Biennial
- Künstlerhaus Bethanien, Berlin

## Residency Project

During his residency at quartier21, Papadopoulos tested ideas on money, its cultural significance, and aesthetics in dialogue with Artistic Bokeh's research on technology and arts.

### Research Focus
- Alternatives to official monetary systems
- Proliferation of cryptocurrencies (post-Bitcoin success)
- Aesthetic qualities of code and its relation to value/money
- Immaterialization and its impact on visual fields

### Collaborative Installation: "Too Much Money"
In collaboration with the Parisian cooperative **Société Réaliste**, Papadopoulos developed the installation "Too Much Money" which opened on February 27, 2014 in the Electric Avenue of MuseumsQuartier.

**Installation Concept**: A commentary on the relation between money, value, and artistic practice.

**Opening Events**:
- Screening of the film *Art Accounts Deutsche Bank* (2013) by Carsten Lisecki
- Series of small events and discussions
