## About the Artist

**Toby Heys (aka Battery Operated)** is a media artist from Manchester, UK. As an artist-in-residence with 5uper.net, he worked on the project "SPIRAWL" - a web-based documentary and CD/DVD project exploring sound weapons and sonic landscapes.

### Residency Project: SPIRAWL
The project investigates how sound and music are used as weapons by military and civil enforcement organizations. It examines:
- **Sound weapons**: Devices like the Long Range Acoustic Device (LRAD) capable of 150 decibels
- **Infrasound weapons**: Frequencies under 20 Hz that can cause nausea, organ damage, and death
- **Sonic landscapes**: The mapping and regulation of audible sound fields
- **Democratic rights**: Questions about democracy in a landscape where invisible sound weapons operate

The documentary asks: "What does democracy sound like?" and explores the ethical implications of non-lethal sound weapons tested in war zones and later used on civilian populations.
