# [Artist-in-residence] SETUP.NL – Ryanne Turenhout, Frank-Jan van Lunteren, Tijmen Schep, Danielle de Jonge, Max Laane [NL]

## Event content (extracted)
Source capture includes bilingual DE/EN event description for **"Artistic Technology Research: Meet SETUP.NL"** (Metalab Wien, 19 Nov 2012), with background on SETUP’s mission, Utrecht context, and the focus on social/cultural implications of technology.

### Extract (DE)
"Einladung zur Veranstaltung // MEET SETUP.NL // ... Gute Ideen kommen nicht immer von institutionalisierten Forschungs & Entwicklungsabteilungen ... Hacker-Spaces, Kunstlabs und DIY-Communities ... Bei dem Treffen mit dem Metalab soll die Rolle von Freiräumen zur Entwicklung von Technik, zum Experiment und zur kritischen Befragung erörtert werden."

### Extract (EN)
"SETUP is Utrecht's stage for everyone interested in combining technology and creative culture ... it does not focus on the technology itself so much, but rather is concerned with social implications, cultural meaning and creative ways of putting new technologies to use ..."
