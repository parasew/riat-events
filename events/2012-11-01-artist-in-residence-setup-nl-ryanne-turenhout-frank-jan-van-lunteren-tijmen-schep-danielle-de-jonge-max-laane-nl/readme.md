# Artist-in-residence: SETUP.NL – Ryanne Turenhout, Frank-Jan van Lunteren, Tijmen Schep, Danielle de Jonge, Max Laane [NL]

## Event Details

- **Date**: November 1, 2012 (artist-in-residence start), with discussion event on November 19, 2012
- **Event**: "Artistic Technology Research: Meet SETUP.NL"
- **Location**: Metalab Wien, Vienna
- **Organizers**: Artistic Technology Research / Artistic Bokeh / quartier21
- **Type**: Artist-in-Residence presentation and discussion
- **Participants**: 5 members of SETUP.NL from Utrecht, Netherlands
- **Context**: Part of quartier21 artist-in-residence program at MuseumsQuartier Vienna

## Event Description

### Bilingual Event Announcement (DE/EN)

#### German Version
"Einladung zur Veranstaltung // MEET SETUP.NL // ... Gute Ideen kommen nicht immer von institutionalisierten Forschungs & Entwicklungsabteilungen ... Hacker-Spaces, Kunstlabs und DIY-Communities ... Bei dem Treffen mit dem Metalab soll die Rolle von Freiräumen zur Entwicklung von Technik, zum Experiment und zur kritischen Befragung erörtert werden."

#### English Version
"SETUP is Utrecht's stage for everyone interested in combining technology and creative culture ... it does not focus on the technology itself so much, but rather is concerned with social implications, cultural meaning and creative ways of putting new technologies to use ..."

## About SETUP.NL

### Organization Profile
**SETUP.NL** is a Utrecht-based organization that serves as:
- **Platform for technology and creative culture**: Connecting artists, technologists, and researchers
- **Critical technology discourse**: Focusing on social and cultural implications of technology
- **Community space**: Supporting hacker-spaces, art labs, and DIY communities
- **Experimental platform**: Exploring creative applications of new technologies

### Mission and Approach
SETUP.NL distinguishes itself by:
- **Focus on implications**: Rather than just technology itself, focusing on social and cultural impacts
- **Interdisciplinary approach**: Bridging technology, art, and social discourse
- **Community engagement**: Creating spaces for experimentation and critical inquiry
- **International collaboration**: Partnering with similar organizations across Europe

## Discussion Themes

### Role of Independent Spaces
The event explored the role of:
- **Hacker-spaces and art labs** as alternatives to institutional R&D
- **DIY communities** in technology development
- **Freiräume (free spaces)** for experimentation and critical inquiry
- **Independent platforms** for technology discourse

### Artistic Technology Research
Discussion focused on:
- **Social implications** of technology
- **Cultural meaning** of technological development
- **Creative applications** of new technologies
- **Critical perspectives** on technological innovation
