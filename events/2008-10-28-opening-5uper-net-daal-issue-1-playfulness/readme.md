## Description

"PLAYFULNESS" describes the human tendency toward playfulness as found in LUDIC INTERFACES or DEVICE MODS. Playfulness is defined here both through the pleasure and curiosity that humans have toward technological innovations, as well as through the DIY ethos that is strongly pronounced in these disciplines.

This event serves as a precursor to the CODED CULTURES festival taking place in May and October 2009 in Vienna and Tokyo.

## Context

Part of the CODED CULTURES festival exploring creative emergences at the intersection of media arts, technology, and cultural production. The event examines artistic-creative production mechanisms shaped by digitally networked and media-cultural organizational forms.

## About DAAL

The DAAL (Digital Arts and Architecture Lab) project represents a structure for research and development, experimental architecture and prototype development in the fields of art, architecture, design and technology, operating its research and development facility in Vienna's Arsenal.
