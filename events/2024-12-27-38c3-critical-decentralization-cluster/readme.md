RIAT and decentral.community co-organized the Critical Decentralisation Cluster (CDC) at the 38th Chaos Communication Congress (38C3) in Hamburg during December 27-30, 2024. The CDC is a group of likeminded assemblies working together to present projects, talks and workshops that critically discuss the future of decentralisation.

The CDC was part of the Community Content Team of 38C3 and helped put together the Community Stages program. The cluster also hosted its own talks and workshops in the CDC assembly space.

## Participating Assemblies

The CDC at 38C3 included the following assemblies and projects:

- **RIAT** - independent research institute in Vienna
- **MoneroKon** and **Monero Community** - privacy-focused cryptocurrency and conference
- **Replicant** - fully free Android distribution
- **Namecoin** - decentralized tamper-resistant naming system + PKI
- **GNU Boot** - de-shittification for your BIOS and UEFI
- **Social Dist0rtion Protocol** - ad-hoc riddles and games
- **FOSSASIA** - open-source community in Asia
- **Swiss Cryptoeconomics** - research collective
- **Department of Decentralisation** - collective from Berlin
- **Ethereum Foundation** - non-profit supporting the Ethereum ecosystem
- **Reticulum** - resilient cryptography-based networking stack
- **Radicle** - peer-to-peer code collaboration stack built on Git
- **Crypto Commons** - P2P technologies for the stewardship of common goods
- **Off-Grid Messaging** - independent networks over LoRa and packet-radio
- **Sibermerdeka** - Kuala Lumpur's up-and-coming hackerspace
- **HSBXL** - Hackerspace Brussels (aka Squirrel)

## Organization & Infrastructure

### Location
The CDC was located in **Hall H, Level 0, I2-K3** at the Congress Center Hamburg (CCH).

### Communication & Coordination
- **Matrix**: Coordination and discussion on `#cdc:dod.ngo`
- **Email**: c3 at RIAT's domain
- **Talk Submissions**: CDC pretalx instance at [https://pretalx.riat.at/38c3/cfp](https://pretalx.riat.at/38c3/cfp)

### Program & Schedule
The CDC featured a comprehensive program with talks and workshops. The full schedule was available via the CDC pretalx instance: [https://pretalx.riat.at/38c3/schedule](https://pretalx.riat.at/38c3/schedule)

## Community Involvement
As part of the 38C3 Community Content Team, the CDC helped curate and organize content for the Community Stages program, contributing to the broader congress experience beyond the dedicated CDC space.
