## Event Content

### RISO Printing Technology
**RISO** (also called Risograph) is:
- **Digital duplicator**: Printing technology combining digital and stencil printing
- **Vibrant colors**: Known for bright, saturated ink colors
- **DIY appeal**: Accessible technology for small-scale publishing
- **Artistic medium**: Used by artists for its distinctive aesthetic qualities

### RISO Convention Activities
The event likely included:
- **Printing demonstrations**: Showing RISO printing process and techniques
- **Workshops**: Hands-on printing sessions for participants
- **Exhibition**: Showcasing RISO-printed artworks and publications
- **Artist presentations**: Artists discussing their RISO-based work
- **Community building**: Connecting Vienna's RISO and printing community

### DIY Publishing Culture
The convention explored:
- **Zine culture**: Self-published magazines and booklets
- **Independent publishing**: Small-scale, artist-run publishing
- **Print activism**: Using printing for political or social expression
- **Community documentation**: Recording and sharing community knowledge through print
