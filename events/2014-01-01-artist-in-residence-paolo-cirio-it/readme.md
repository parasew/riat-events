## About the Artist

**Paolo Cirio** is an Italian conceptual artist based in New York and Turin who works with legal, economic, and cultural systems of the information society. His practice involves hacking, exposing, and subverting opaque power structures through interventions in media, law, and technology.

### Artistic Practice
Cirio's work examines social systems impacted by the Internet, such as privacy, copyright, democracy, and finance. He creates conceptual art projects that often involve:
- **Media interventions**: Hacking and repurposing online platforms
- **Legal art**: Creating alternative legal frameworks and contracts
- **Economic systems**: Exploring cryptocurrency, banking, and financial networks
- **Social engineering**: Exposing vulnerabilities in social and technical systems

### Notable Projects
- **Street Ghosts** (2012): Life-size prints of Google Street View figures installed where they were photographed
- **Loophole for All** (2013): Exposed offshore companies from tax havens
- **Daily Paywall** (2014): Hacked New York Times paywall to make articles free
- **Face to Facebook** (2011): Stole 1 million Facebook profiles and created a dating website

### Exhibitions & Recognition
Cirio has exhibited internationally at venues including:
- Venice Biennale
- Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), New York
- Centre Pompidou, Paris
- Tate Modern, London
- Transmediale, Berlin
- Ars Electronica, Linz

### Awards
- Prix Ars Electronica (Golden Nica), 2014
- Transmediale Award, 2011
- Eyebeam Fellowship, New York
