- **Date**: February 7, 2016
- **Time**: 13:00–15:00
- **Location**: Haus der Kulturen der Welt Berlin, Room K2
- **City**: Berlin, Germany
- **Organizers**: Critical Media Lab Basel & Research Institute for Arts and Technology (RIAT)
- **Type**: Discussion Session / Festival Event
- **Festival**: transmediale – festival for art and digital culture 2016
- **Registration**: Required via Eventbrite
- **Website**: [transmediale program](https://2016.transmediale.de/content/unmaking-5-anxieties)

## About the Event

**"Unmaking: 5 Anxieties"** was a discussion session presented at the 2016 transmediale festival in Berlin, exploring the theme of "Unmaking" as a critical examination of the current state of maker culture, its exhaustion and exploitation.

### Event Concept
The session aimed to foster discussion on the limits and blind spots of the maker attitude and try to develop alternative framings for making and ways of resisting certain imperatives of creativity.

### Collaboration
The event was a collaboration between:
- **Critical Media Lab Basel** (Institute of Experimental Design and Media Cultures, FHNW Academy of Art and Design)
- **Research Institute for Arts and Technology (RIAT)** Vienna

## The Unmaking Kit

As part of the collaboration, RIAT and Critical Media Lab conceived an **"Unmaking Kit"** – a limited edition publication in card deck format intended to trigger conversation at the festival.

### Kit Features
- **Format**: Card deck publication
- **Purpose**: To stimulate discussion and critical reflection
- **Availability**: Distributed in the lower passage of HKW during the opening night and festival days

## Participants

### Discussion Leaders
- Representatives from **Critical Media Lab Basel**
- Representatives from **Research Institute for Arts and Technology (RIAT)**

### Special Guest
- **Tom Jenkins** – Theorist, designer, and PhD researcher at Georgia Tech

## Discussion Themes

### Critical Examination of Maker Culture
- **Exhaustion of maker culture**: Critical perspectives on the maker movement
- **Exploitation concerns**: Labor, commodification, and neoliberal co-optation
- **Limits of maker attitude**: Blind spots and limitations in maker philosophy

### Alternative Framings
- **Resisting creativity imperatives**: Alternative approaches to making
- **Unmaking as practice**: Deconstruction, critique, and alternative methodologies
- **Critical design perspectives**: Beyond solutionism and techno-optimism

## Context: transmediale 2016

### Festival Theme: conversation piece
The 2016 transmediale festival focused on "conversation piece" – exploring conversation as artistic and critical practice in digital culture.
