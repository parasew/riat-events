The *Decentralized Internet and Privacy Devroom* at FOSDEM 2026 featured talks from technical projects and initiatives fostering decentralization and privacy on the Internet.

The devroom took place on February 1st, 2026, and covered topics such as ad-hoc and mesh networks, cryptography-based network stacks, anonymous overlay networks, and privacy-preserving browsers.
