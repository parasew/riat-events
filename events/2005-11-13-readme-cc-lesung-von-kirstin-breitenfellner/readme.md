## About readme.cc

readme.cc is a literary event series where experts, critics, writers, or cultural figures select and read from a book they personally recommend. The series creates a space for literary discovery and discussion, connecting readers with books through personal recommendations from knowledgeable individuals.

## Featured Reader: Kirstin Breitenfellner

Kirstin Breitenfellner is likely an Austrian writer, critic, or cultural figure who selects and reads from a book of personal significance. The event provides insight into her literary tastes and recommendations.

## Series Context

This event is part of the ongoing readme.cc monthly reading series that began on June 26, 2005. The series represents a collaboration between 5uper.net and readme.cc to create regular literary programming within the cultural space of the Cuisine Digitale.

## Format

Each readme.cc event typically features:
- Introduction of the guest reader and their literary background
- Reading from the selected book
- Discussion about why the book was chosen
- Q&A session with the audience
- Opportunity for audience members to discover new literature through personal recommendations
