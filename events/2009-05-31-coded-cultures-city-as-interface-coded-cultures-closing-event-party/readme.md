# [Coded Cultures: City as Interface] Coded Cultures Closing Event / Party

## Details

- **Date:** May 31, 2009
- **Time:** Evening
- **Location:** MQ Hofstallungen (MUMOK), MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net / CODED CULTURES
- **Type:** Festival Closing Party
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/173.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20150209174810/https://5uper.net/post/173.html)

## Description

The closing event of the CODED CULTURES festival in Vienna, featuring:

1. **Pecha Kucha Night** - Presentations by young designers and architects (20 images × 20 seconds each)
2. **Live Performance by Tetsuya Umeda** - Unique sound-performances using everyday objects and electro-acoustic elements
3. **Contemporary electronic music** featuring live-acts and DJs from Trust Records and the Japanese party format Minimal Tokyo
4. **Visuals by DEXTRO**

## Program

### Pecha Kucha Night
Devised by Astrid Klein and Mark Dytham (Klein Dytham architecture) in Tokyo, this format allows each presenter 20 images shown for 20 seconds each (6 minutes 40 seconds total).

### Tetsuya Umeda
Emerging artist from Japan known for his unique sound-performances using everyday objects and subtle electro-acoustic elements. He has participated in numerous exhibitions including "Festival Beyond Innocence" (Osaka), "Instal" (Scotland), and "Sound Effect Seoul".

### Minimal Tokyo & Trust Records
- **Minimal Tokyo:** International collective of DJs, producers, designers and media artists running one of Tokyo's top events for electronic dance music with minimal techno, click house and tech house.
- **Trust Records:** Vienna's main outlet for Detroit-style underground electro, techno bass and futuristic beats, run by DJ Glow.

## Context

Part of the CODED CULTURES festival exploring creative emergences at the intersection of media arts, technology, and urban culture.
