The Critical Decentralisation Cluster (CDC) returned to the 39th Chaos Communication Congress (39C3) in Hamburg during December 27-30, 2025. The CDC brought together assemblies and projects that challenge centralized infrastructures with decentralised, privacy-preserving and open hardware approaches. The cluster featured talks, workshops, and hallway conversations around cypherpunk futures.

## Participating Assemblies

The CDC at 39C3 included the following assemblies and projects:

- **RIAT** - independent research institute in Vienna
- **Monero Community** - privacy-focused cryptocurrency
- **Namecoin** - decentralized tamper-resistant naming system + PKI
- **Swiss Cryptoeconomics** - research collective
- **I2P** - fully encrypted peer-to-peer overlay network
- **Cyberraum** - hackers' group from Hamburg
- **Radicle** - peer-to-peer code collaboration stack built on Git
- **Social Dist0rtion Protocol** - ad-hoc riddles and games
- **GNU Boot** - de-shittification for your BIOS and UEFI
- **Department of Decentralisation** - collective from Berlin
- **Firo** - independent blockchain privacy and decentralization tech research
- **Iroh** - library and protocol stacks to build next-gen P2P networks
- **FOSSASIA** - open-source community in Asia
- **Off-Grid Messaging** - independent networks over LoRa and packet-radio
- **Sibermerdeka** - hackerspace in Kuala Lumpur
- **Replicant** - fully free Android distribution
- **Auditable silicon group TROPIC01** - open-source IC designers
- **Reticulum** - resilient cryptography-based networking stack
- **Infinite Build** - peer-support group for builders
- **Crypto Commons** - P2P technologies for the stewardship of common goods
- **BORDEL** - hackerspace in Prague
- **Gosling** - P2P over Tor onion services
- **Cat Pictures Data Distribution Hub** - challenges with cute kitty pictures as rewards
- **Nitrokey** - Open Source IT security hardware
- **XMPP** - the open standard for messaging and presence

## Infrastructure & Organization

### CDC Badge
The CDC featured a custom hardware badge developed by RIAT LABS: [https://github.com/riatlabs/cdc-badge](https://github.com/riatlabs/cdc-badge)

### Location & Layout
The CDC was located in Hall H of the Congress Center Hamburg (CCH). The space included:
- Tables and seats for approximately 120 people
- 2 info tables and meeting spots
- 2 workshop areas (CDC Triangle and CDC Circle)
- Mini-stage with recording equipment in the CDC Triangle
- 3 screens in the CDC Circle (no recording)
