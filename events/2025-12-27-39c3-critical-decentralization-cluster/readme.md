The Critical Decentralisation Cluster (CDC) returned to the 39th Chaos Communication Congress (39C3) in Hamburg.

The CDC brought together assemblies and projects that challenge centralized infrastructures with decentralised, privacy-preserving, and open hardware approaches. The cluster featured talks, workshops, and sessions around cypherpunk futures.

Participating assemblies included RIAT, Monero Community, Namecoin, I2P, Radicle, GNU Boot, and many others.
