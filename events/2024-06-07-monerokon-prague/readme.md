# MoneroKon 2024: Nurturing Decentralization in the Age of Digital Commons

## Summary
Matthias Tarasiewicz presented **Nurturing Decentralization in the Age of Digital Commons** at MoneroKon 2024 in Prague, focusing on sustainability, governance, and social infrastructure for decentralized systems.
