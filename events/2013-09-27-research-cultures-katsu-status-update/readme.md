# [Research Cultures] KATSU – Status Update

## Exhibition Details

- **Exhibition Period**: September 27, 2013 - February 24, 2014
- **Location**: Artistic Bokeh Showroom, quartier21, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Address**: A-1070 Vienna, Museumsplatz 1
- **Organizer**: Artistic Bokeh
- **Type**: Exhibition
- **Context**: Part of the "Faceless II" exhibition around MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Website**: [MQW Event Page](https://www.mqw.at/en/program/programmdetail/katsu-status-update-1/)

## About the Exhibition

**"KATSU: Status Update"** featured work by New York City graffiti artist KATSU, invited by Artistic Bokeh to display his piece "Status Update" in the context of the "Faceless II" exhibition.

### The Artwork
The Artistic Bokeh space was covered with images of a beaten-up Mark Zuckerberg, creating a striking visual commentary on social media, privacy, and digital identity.

### About KATSU
KATSU is a prolific artist, vandal, and hacker who emerged in New York City in the late 1990s. He is known for:
- Tagging with both "KATSU" and a distinctive single-stroke skull icon
- Executing high-profile pieces that have brought him both recognition and notoriety
- Working at the intersection of street art, technology, and social commentary

### Exhibition Context
The work was shown in two locations:
1. **Artistic Bokeh Showroom** - Primary exhibition space
2. **Faceless exhibition** - Additional presentation within MuseumsQuartier Vienna

## Themes and Significance

### Social Media Critique
The "Status Update" piece directly engaged with themes of:
- Social media platforms and their founders
- Privacy and surveillance in digital spaces
- The persona and power of tech industry figures

### Graffiti and Digital Culture
KATSU's work bridges traditional graffiti with digital culture, making him a relevant artist for Artistic Bokeh's exploration of the "electrosphere" and artistic research.

### "Faceless II" Context
The exhibition was part of the larger "Faceless II" exhibition exploring anonymity, identity, and representation in digital culture.
