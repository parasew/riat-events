- **Residency Period**: October 2012
- **Location**: RIAT Vienna / quartier21, MuseumsQuartier

- **Recommending Institution**: ARTISTIC BOKEH

## About the Artist

**Mirko Tobias Schäfer** is a media scholar, researcher, and the first official artist-in-residence of RIAT. Based in Utrecht, Netherlands, he works at the intersection of media studies, digital culture, and artistic research.

### Background
- **Nationality**: Germany, Netherlands
- **Residence**: Utrecht, Netherlands
- **Field**: Media Art, Media Studies, Digital Culture
- **Role**: First RIAT artist-in-residence (October 2012)

### Research Project: "Artistic Technology Research"
Schäfer's residency launched the research project "Artistic Technology Research" at the university (running until 2014, PEEK project funded by FWF - Austrian Science Fund).

**Project Focus**: 
- Investigation of innovation and knowledge transfer in new media art
- Artistic and scientific examination of media art practices
- Exploration of different formats for presenting and illustrating research

### Residency Activities
As the first RIAT artist-in-residence, Schäfer's October-November 2012 residency included:
- Kickoff events in cooperation with MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- Launch of the "Artistic Technology Research" project
- Establishment of media art research methodologies
- Collaboration with Artistic Bokeh/RIAT research team

### Significance
Mirko Tobias Schäfer's residency marked the formal beginning of RIAT's artist-in-residence program, establishing a framework for future artistic research collaborations at the intersection of art, technology, and media studies.
