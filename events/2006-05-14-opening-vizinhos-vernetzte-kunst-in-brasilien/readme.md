# Opening: VIZINHOS – vernetzte Kunst in Brasilien

## Description

"VIZINHOS: Vernetzte Kunst in Brasilien" (Neighbors: Networked Art in Brazil) is an exhibition presented by the group 5uper.net in the Freiraum of MuseumsQuartier Vienna. The exhibition showcases positions exploring the synergy between Situationism, media art and architecture from the Brazilian art project BASE.

## Context

Timed with the 4th EU-Latin America Summit in Vienna, 5uper.net invited five Brazilian artists through the MuseumsQuartier Wien's Artist in Residence program. In addition to the exhibition, open workshops and research areas in the "5uper.net Cuisine Digitale" provide insights into the production processes of Brazilian artists. Theme-specific film screenings and music events complete the program.

## The BASE Project

BASE was created in January 2002 in northeastern Brazil as an experimental field for media art and visual arts: a house as a site for production, presentation and public discourse. Within two years, communication and networking among artists across the country was strengthened, and the house—called BASE—became a hub for Brazil's young art movement. During this period, exhibitions, film screenings, discussion rounds and lectures by artists from Fortaleza, São Paulo, Brasilia, Recife and Rio de Janeiro were realized.

## Title Significance

"VIZINHOS" (Portuguese for "neighbors") refers to:
1. The networking between Austria and Brazil through cooperation
2. The Brazilian BASE project that created a hub for young Brazilian art movement
3. The focus of most works presented, which deal with networking of social and public spaces

## Opening Event

For the vernissage, Brazilian musician **Mauricio Takara** performed. Takara has been a fixture in the Brazilian music scene for many years, touring Europe (including Sonar Festival Barcelona, Roskilde Denmark, Womex Sevilla & Club Transmediale Berlin), USA, India (World Social Forum) and Brazil. His unconventional style combines percussion, guitar, bass, keyboard, xylophone, triangle and electronic elements, creating a colorful sound formation of dub, jazz, breakbeat and post-rock.
