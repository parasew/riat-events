## Presentation Content

### ETHLend Platform
**ETHLend** was an early decentralized lending platform on Ethereum featuring:
- **Peer-to-peer lending**: Direct lending between individuals without intermediaries
- **Collateralized loans**: Crypto assets as collateral for loans
- **Smart contract execution**: Automated loan agreements and enforcement
- **Global access**: Permissionless lending available to anyone with internet access
