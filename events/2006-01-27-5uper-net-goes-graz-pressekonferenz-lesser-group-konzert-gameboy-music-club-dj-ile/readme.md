# 5uper.net goes Graz! Pressekonferenz LESSER GROUP, Konzert GAMEBOY MUSIC CLUB, DJ ILE

## Details

- **Opening Date:** January 27, 2006
- **Exhibition Duration:** January 27 - February 10, 2006
- **Time:** 19:00
- **Location:** Lendkai 1, 8020 Graz
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net / medien.KUNSTLABOR Kunsthaus Graz / Lesser Group
- **Type:** Exhibition Opening & Concert
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/138.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20060203012501/http://5uper.net/post/138)

## Description

Exhibition and work presentation under new auspices: medien.KUNSTLABOR Kunsthaus Graz presents 5uper.net in cooperation with Lesser Group. A food for thought on the tightrope walk between art and commerce.

## Program

### 1. Press Conference: LESSER GROUP
- **Time:** 19:00
- Presentation of the collaboration between 5uper.net and Lesser Group
- Discussion of the exhibition concept and themes

### 2. Concert: GAMEBOY MUSIC CLUB
- Performance following the press conference
- Likely featuring chiptune or electronic music created with Game Boy systems

### 3. DJ Set: ILE
- Closing DJ set after the concert
- Continuing the musical program into the night

## Exhibition Theme

The event explores "the tightrope walk between art and commerce" - examining the relationship and tensions between artistic practice and commercial interests. This theme is particularly relevant given the cooperation with Lesser Group, which appears to be a corporate or commercial partner.

## Venue

The event takes place at **medien.KUNSTLABOR Kunsthaus Graz** (media.ARTLABOR Art House Graz) at Lendkai 1, 8020 Graz. This represents 5uper.net's expansion beyond Vienna to other Austrian cultural centers.

## Partners

- **5uper.net** - Vienna-based media art organization
- **medien.KUNSTLABOR Kunsthaus Graz** - Graz-based media art laboratory and exhibition space
- **Lesser Group** - Corporate/commercial partner sponsoring the event

## Context

This event represents 5uper.net's outreach to Graz, Austria's second-largest city and an important cultural center. The collaboration with medien.KUNSTLABOR Kunsthaus Graz expands 5uper.net's network and brings its programming to a new audience.
