# [Research Cultures] Apertus & Artistic Bokeh: Open Source Cinema Lab

## Event Details

- **Date**: September 24, 2014
- **Time**: 19:00
- **Location**: Artistic Bokeh Showroom, Electronic Avenue, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Address**: 1070 Vienna, Austria
- **Organizers**: Artistic Bokeh in cooperation with Apertus Open Source Cinema and Artistic Technology Research (University of Applied Arts Vienna)
- **Type**: Exhibition Opening
- **Website**: [MQW Event Page](https://www.mqw.at/programm/programmdetail/apertus-axiom-open-source-cinema-lab/)

## About the Event

Artistic Bokeh in cooperation with Apertus Open Source Cinema and Artistic Technology Research (University of Applied Arts) presented the exhibition "Open Source Cinema Lab" at quartier21 in MuseumsQuartier Vienna.

### Opening Event
The opening on September 24, 2014 provided the opportunity to:
- Meet the Apertus team
- See the Apertus AXIOM alpha camera prototype
- Celebrate the ongoing Indiegogo crowdfunding campaign for the AXIOM Beta camera

### Exhibition Concept
**Open Source Cinema Lab** examines the emerging 'third industrial revolution' occurring within the garages and workshops of enthusiasts and makers. The exhibition highlights how the maker movement is causing both technological changes in industrial manufacturing and a restructuring of basic market principles.

### Apertus Open Source Cinema Project
Apertus endorses freedom through open source principles:
- **Freedom to use**: For any purpose
- **Freedom to study**: Understand how it works
- **Freedom to modify**: Adapt and improve
- **Freedom to redistribute**: Share copies and modified versions

This openness liberates makers, cultural producers, and artists to experiment and create in previously unimagined ways.

### Apertus AXIOM Camera
The Apertus AXIOM is being developed as the world's first publicly available open source digital cinema camera. The exhibition featured the alpha prototype and promoted the crowdfunding campaign for the Beta version.

## Context and Significance

### Maker Movement Impact
The exhibition positioned the Apertus project within the broader maker movement, which has gained significant attention from governments and institutions (notably, Barack Obama hosted a Maker Faire at the White House around this time).

### Artistic Bokeh's Role
Artistic Bokeh sought to draw attention to this groundbreaking project and encourage public contribution to the crowdfunding campaign, supporting the development of open tools for artistic and cultural production.

### Connection to Artistic Technology Research
The event was part of the Artistic Technology Research project at the University of Applied Arts Vienna, exploring the intersection of art, technology, and open source development.
