RIAT and decentral.community co-organized the Critical Decentralisation Cluster at 37C3.

## Event Description

The Critical Decentralisation Cluster at the 37th Chaos Communication Congress (37C3) was an area and grouping of similar minded projects, which offered workshops and hosted a space to critically discuss the future of decentralization.

At 37C3 the congress was back in Hamburg, and the cluster was located in the eastern corner of hall 3 on the ground floor ([c:0:135.5:159.25](https://37c3.c3nav.de/l/c:0:135.5:159.25/)).

## Cluster Features

- **3 workshop areas** for assemblies and similar minded groups/projects
- **Coffee area** for networking and discussions
- **Hacking/working/learning space**
- **Recorded talks session** in Saal D: [CDC Critical Decentralization Cluster Cluster Reco](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2023/hub/en/event/cdc-critical-decentralization-cluster-cluster-reco/)
