# [Research Cultures] Jai McKenzie: Never Forever

## Exhibition Details

- **Exhibition Period**: November 22, 2014 - February 20, 2015
- **Location**: Artistic Bokeh Showroom, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Organizers**: Artistic Bokeh and Austria Australia Arts Alliance
- **Type**: Exhibition
- **Artist**: Jai McKenzie (Australian artist based in Sydney and Berlin)
- **Website**: [quartier21 Event Page](https://www.quartier21.at/programm/programmdetail/jai-mckenzie-aus-never-forever/)

## About the Exhibition

**"Never Forever"** is an exhibition by Australian artist Jai McKenzie that examines the dynamic journey of creation and destruction, the act of remembering and forgetting, and the improbability of permanence in an inherently impermanent world.

### Inspiration
The exhibition was conceived after the artist visited the **Nakagin Capsule Tower** in Tokyo's Shimbashi district. Designed by Metabolist architect Kisho Kurokawa and built over just 30 days in 1972, the Capsule Tower is composed of 140 inhabitable pods designed to be continuously removed, re-arranged, and ultimately destroyed. Today, the pods are largely unoccupied and wait in varying states of decay for impending demolition.

### Artistic Concept
McKenzie's work exists as if she sifted through the remains of the building to compile a rearrangement of objects repositioned within time, space, and imagination. Various forms are eroded through light and fragmented through space.

### Materials and Approach
McKenzie's interest in perceptual experience often results in sculptures and installations that use **light, space, and time** as the principal materials.

## About the Artist

### Jai McKenzie
- **Based in**: Sydney, Australia and Berlin, Germany
- **Position**: Lecturer in Electronic and New Media Art at Sydney College of the Arts, University of Sydney
- **Education**: Doctorate of Philosophy (Studio-based Research in Art) from Sydney College of the Arts, University of Sydney
- **Awards**: Australian Post Graduate Award, Australia Council New Work grant, National Association of Visual Arts artist grant, Fauvette Loureiro Memorial Artists Exchange Scholarship

### Exhibition History
McKenzie's work has been exhibited extensively across Australia as well as in Japan, Germany, Austria, Canada, and the United States.

### Publications
- **Book**: *Light and Photomedia* (2014) - Places the reader in a new history and future of photomedia theory, offering essential lines of enquiry for contemporary thinking in photography.

### Residencies and Experience
- Participation in residency programs in Sydney and Paris
- Over five years of lecturing experience at University of Technology Sydney, Sydney College of the Arts, and University of Newcastle

## Context

### Austria Australia Arts Alliance
This exhibition was the first event of the **Austria Australia Arts Alliance (AAAA)** exhibition program at Artistic Bokeh. AAAA was established to present and promote emerging experimental Australian artists in Austria.

### Vienna Art Week 2014
The exhibition opening was part of **Vienna Art Week 2014**, connecting Australian experimental art with the Vienna art scene.

"Never Forever" represented the beginning of the Austria Australia Arts Alliance program at Artistic Bokeh, fostering international artistic exchange and presenting Australian experimental art in Vienna. The exhibition explored themes of impermanence, memory, and architectural decay through light-based installations and sculptural works.
