# [Making Artistic Technology] LIMEN Workshop with Mario de Vega and Víctor Mazón Gardoqui

## Event Details

- **Date**: April 21, 2015
- **Event**: LIMEN Workshop
- **Series**: Making Artistic Technology workshop series
- **Workshop Leaders**: Mario de Vega and Víctor Mazón Gardoqui
- **Organizer**: Artistic Technology Research / Artistic Bokeh
- **Location**: Artistic Technology Research / Artistic Bokeh space, Vienna
- **Type**: Hands-on workshop combining technology, art, and critical inquiry

## Workshop Description

### English Version
"LIMEN is a workshop about data traffic and wireless telecommunication where participants build an analogue portable device that makes electromagnetic activity (Bluetooth, WLAN, mobile phones, GPS, microwaves) audible."

### German Version
"LIMEN ist ein Workshop über Datenverkehr und drahtlose Telekommunikation, in dem Teilnehmende ein analoges, tragbares Gerät bauen, das elektromagnetische Aktivität (Bluetooth, WLAN, Mobiltelefone, GPS, Mikrowellen) hörbar macht."

## Workshop Concept

### Making the Invisible Audible
LIMEN (Latin for "threshold" or "boundary") focused on:
- **Revealing electromagnetic spectrum**: Making invisible wireless communications audible
- **DIY device building**: Creating portable analogue detection devices
- **Critical awareness**: Understanding the pervasive nature of wireless signals
- **Sensory experience**: Translating digital data traffic into sound

### Technical Approach
Participants built analogue devices that could detect and sonify:
- **Bluetooth signals**
- **Wi-Fi networks (WLAN)**
- **Mobile phone communications**
- **GPS satellite signals**
- **Microwave emissions**

## Workshop Leaders

### Mario de Vega
- **Background**: Sound artist and researcher
- **Focus**: Experimental sound, electromagnetic phenomena, critical technology
- **Approach**: Combining technical skill with artistic and critical perspectives

### Víctor Mazón Gardoqui
- **Background**: Artist and technologist
- **Focus**: DIY electronics, open hardware, experimental interfaces
- **Approach**: Hands-on technical workshops with critical dimensions

## Context: Making Artistic Technology Series

### Workshop Series Philosophy
The "Making Artistic Technology" series aimed to:
- **Demystify technology**: Make technical processes accessible
- **Combine making and thinking**: Integrate hands-on skills with critical reflection
- **Foster interdisciplinary learning**: Bridge art, technology, and social inquiry
- **Build community**: Create spaces for collaborative learning and experimentation

### Typical Workshop Structure
- **Technical instruction**: Hands-on building and making
- **Conceptual framework**: Critical and theoretical context
- **Collaborative work**: Group learning and problem-solving
- **Discussion and reflection**: Connecting technical practice to broader implications

## Workshop Content and Activities

### Device Building
Participants learned to construct:
- **Analogue electromagnetic detectors**: Simple, portable devices
- **Signal processing circuits**: Converting electromagnetic waves to sound
- **Portable systems**: Creating wearable or carry-able detection tools

### Signal Exploration
The workshop included:
- **Mapping wireless landscapes**: Identifying different signal sources
- **Comparative listening**: Distinguishing between signal types
- **Environmental awareness**: Understanding electromagnetic environments
- **Critical discussion**: Social and political dimensions of wireless infrastructure

## Theoretical and Critical Dimensions

### Electromagnetic Awareness
The workshop explored:
- **Invisible infrastructures**: The hidden networks of wireless communication
- **Environmental impact**: The proliferation of electromagnetic signals
- **Privacy implications**: Surveillance and data collection through wireless networks
- **Sensory politics**: Who controls and experiences electromagnetic spaces

### Artistic Technology Approach
LIMEN exemplified the "Artistic Technology" methodology:
- **Technical skill development**: Learning practical electronics
- **Artistic expression**: Creating sensory experiences from data
- **Critical inquiry**: Questioning technological systems and their impacts
- **Community engagement**: Collaborative learning and knowledge sharing

## Significance

### Critical Technology Education
The LIMEN workshop represented:
- **Accessible technology education**: Making complex concepts approachable
- **Critical making practice**: Combining technical skills with social awareness
- **Interdisciplinary learning**: Bridging art, technology, and critical theory
- **Community knowledge sharing**: Collaborative approach to technology education

### Artistic Technology Research Legacy
As part of the "Making Artistic Technology" series, LIMEN contributed to:
- **Developing pedagogical approaches** for technology art education
- **Building technical communities** around critical making practices
- **Documenting workshop methodologies** for future reference
- **Establishing Vienna** as a center for critical technology education

The workshop exemplified the hands-on, critical approach that characterized Artistic Technology Research / Artistic Bokeh's educational programming in the mid-2010s.
