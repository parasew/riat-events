# Awards for Research in Artistic Technology

## Event Details

- **Date**: December 10, 2015
- **Location**: Gartenbaukino, Vienna
- **Organizer**: Research Institute for Arts and Technology (RIAT)
- **Type**: Award Ceremony
- **Facebook Event**: [696164823852719](https://www.facebook.com/events/696164823852719/)
- **MQW Event Page**: [Awards for Research in Artistic Technology](https://www.mqw.at/en/program/programmdetail/awards-for-research-in-artistic-technology-1/)

## About the Event

The **"Awards for Research in Artistic Technology"** ceremony recognized and celebrated outstanding research at the intersection of arts and technology. The event was organized by RIAT (Research Institute for Arts and Technology) at the historic Gartenbaukino cinema in Vienna.

### Event Significance
This award ceremony represented RIAT's commitment to promoting and recognizing innovative research that bridges artistic practice and technological innovation. It highlighted the institute's role in fostering interdisciplinary research communities.

## Gartenbaukino Venue

### Historical Significance
- **Gartenbaukino**: One of Vienna's oldest and most prestigious cinemas
- **Location**: Parkring 12, 1010 Vienna
- **History**: Opened in 1960, known for its modernist architecture and cultural significance
- **Context**: A fitting venue for celebrating artistic and technological innovation

## Award Categories and Focus

### Research in Artistic Technology
The awards likely recognized research that:
- **Integrates arts and technology**: Innovative combinations of artistic practice with technological research
- **Advances methodological approaches**: New research methods at the art-technology intersection
- **Produces significant outcomes**: Research with impact in both artistic and technological domains
- **Fosters interdisciplinary collaboration**: Work that bridges different fields and communities

### Potential Award Areas

- **Artistic technology development**

- **Experimental research practices**

## RIAT's Role in Artistic Technology Research

### Institutional Context
In 2015, RIAT was actively engaged in:
- **Artistic research projects**: Supporting research at the art-technology intersection
- **Interdisciplinary collaborations**: Working across arts, technology, and science
- **Research dissemination**: Sharing research outcomes through events and publications
- **Community building**: Fostering networks of artists, researchers, and technologists

### Evolution of Focus
This 2015 event represented RIAT's focus on "Arts and Technology," which would later evolve toward "Future Cryptoeconomics" by 2018, reflecting the institute's adaptation to emerging technological and cultural landscapes.
