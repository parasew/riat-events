# Start: CC – Exploring Creative Emergences

## Details

- **Date:** May 28 – June 7, 2009
- **Location:** Freiraum/quartier21, MuseumsQuartier Vienna
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net / CODED CULTURES
- **Type:** Exhibition
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/172.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20150209174810/https://5uper.net/post/172.html)

## Description

The exhibition "CODED CULTURES - Exploring Creative Emergences" presents international artworks and projects related to the four sub-topics of the festival:

1. **Designing Complexity**
2. **Assembling Things**
3. **Expanding Locality**
4. **Creating Proto-Culture**

The presented positions from Japan, Austria and Europe are distinguished by creative practices which explore new forms of media integrated delineation- and knowledge-cultures. They are situated at the intersection of disciplines and have actively integrated different cultural fields in their artistic development.
