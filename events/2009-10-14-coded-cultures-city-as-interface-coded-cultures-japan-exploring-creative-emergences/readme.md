# [Coded Cultures: City as Interface] Coded Cultures Japan – Exploring Creative Emergences

## Details

- **Date:** October 14-18, 2009
- **Location:** Yokohama and Tokyo, Japan
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net
- **Type:** International Festival
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/178.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20150209174810/https://5uper.net/post/178.html)
- **Festival Site:** [codedcultures.net](http://codedcultures.net)

## Description

Coded Cultures Japan is a binational festival examining artistic-creative skill profiles in media-integrated design cultures. The festival focuses on a new generation of media and design cultures and their creative practices. Diverse scenes and projects that create new application areas using electronic media are networked and made accessible to outsiders. The festival thus addresses cultural development processes at the intersection of art, technology, science, and entertainment.

## Context

As part of the official "Austria-Japan Year 2009", Coded Cultures presents itself as a creative mediation platform in both countries. 140 years of diplomatic relations provide the occasion for presenting developmental connections between "Creative Cities" like Vienna and Yokohama.

## Festival Themes

The four thematic areas of the festival convey content, forms, and functions of cultural practices on different levels, showing developments from creative vision to concrete virtuality:

1. **Designing Complexity**
2. **Assembling Things**
3. **Expanding Locality**
4. **Creating Proto-Culture**

## Significance

The festival represents the Japanese edition of the CODED CULTURES festival that took place in Vienna in May 2009, creating a binational dialogue between Austrian and Japanese media art scenes within the framework of the Austria-Japan Year 2009 celebrations.
