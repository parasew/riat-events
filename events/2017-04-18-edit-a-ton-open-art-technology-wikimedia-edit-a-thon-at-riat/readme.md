## Event Content

### Wikipedia Edit-a-thon Format
An edit-a-thon typically includes:
- **Wikipedia training**: Teaching editing skills to participants
- **Topic selection**: Identifying articles that need improvement or creation
- **Collaborative editing**: Group work on improving Wikipedia content
- **Research support**: Providing resources and references for article improvement
- **Community building**: Connecting Wikipedia editors with shared interests

## Context: Wikipedia and Open Knowledge

### Wikipedia's Importance
Wikipedia is:
- **Major knowledge resource**: One of world's most visited websites
- **Community-driven**: Created and maintained by volunteer editors
- **Open knowledge model**: Free access and open editing philosophy
- **Coverage gaps**: Some topics, especially niche or emerging fields, are underrepresented

### Edit-a-thon Movement
Edit-a-thons have become popular for:
- **Addressing biases**: Improving coverage of underrepresented topics and perspectives
- **Community engagement**: Bringing experts together to share knowledge
- **Skill development**: Teaching people how to contribute to Wikipedia
- **Institutional outreach**: Organizations contributing to public knowledge resources
