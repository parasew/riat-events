# Cuisine Digitale Closing Party

## Details

- **Date:** July 8, 2006
- **Time:** From 19:30
- **Location:** 5uper.net Cuisine Digitale, Freiraum MuseumsQuartier Wien
- **Organizer:** 5uper.net
- **Type:** Closing Party & Event
- **Website:** [5uper.net/post/156.html](https://5uper.net/post/156.html)

## Description

With August 2006, the exhibition operation in the Cuisine Digitale will be discontinued. Before that, there will be a proper celebration.

## About Cuisine Digitale

The project CUISINE DIGITALE by the artist group 5uper.net is a space in MuseumsQuartier Vienna. Over the past two years, there have been regular events, screenings and meetings for producers and interested parties in the fields of media, art, architecture and music.

Despite the great success and the many events, the exhibition operation in the Cuisine Digitale will be discontinued from August. In the last half year, the group has developed more strongly in terms of content, our focus will be on our own projects in the future. There will continue to be actions and events by 5uper.net, but no longer only in the MuseumsQuartier.

## Historical Context

To date, over 70 events (including exhibitions, workshops and openings) have been realized and presented by 5uper.net. The financing was borne by the individuals of the group, there was hardly any funding.

Over 100 artists have worked with 5uper.net in cooperations, including international artists from Austria, Japan, Germany, UK, France, Italy, Slovenia, Mexico, Brazil, Sweden, Serbia, Netherlands, Croatia, Russia, Bulgaria, Hungary, Macedonia, and more.
