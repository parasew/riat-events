# Dark Prague 2025: Verifiable or Void — Hacking Our Way Back to Sovereign Computing

## Summary
Matthias Tarasiewicz (RIAT) presented a talk on **sovereign computing** and the risks of “tethered appliances,” arguing for verifiable hardware, open systems, and cultural practices of appropriation. The talk connects cypherpunk values, open hardware, and the end‑of‑ownership debate to a roadmap toward resilient, user‑controlled computing.

## Key themes
- The “end of ownership” and the rise of rented, locked‑down devices
- Open hardware and firmware as a path to verifiability
- Cypherpunk values: not just writing code, but **building and using verifiable hardware**
- Cultures of appropriation and sovereign computing devices
